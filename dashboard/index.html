What this index measures

The Care Quotient (CQ) measures care capacity — the extent to which a city has the social ties, institutions, and infrastructure needed to support people in moments of vulnerability. This is explicitly not a quality-of-life index. A city can score well on income or safety while having thin care infrastructure for its most vulnerable residents.

The index is organized into two pillars: Social Support & Connection measures whether people are embedded in networks that can provide support. Institutions of Care measures whether organizations exist that are specifically designed to absorb distress.

Each metric is scored against an absolute benchmark — the level at which a city would be considered to fully meet that dimension of care need. A score of 100 means the benchmark is met; no current city achieves it on every metric. Full methodology →

V1 covers 5 cities. V2 will expand to 100 cities and add a Responsiveness pillar measuring whether systems act when people reach out.
